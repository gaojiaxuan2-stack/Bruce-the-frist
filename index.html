    Particle 3D

    Interactive particle worlds you control with your hand.
    Hold your hand up in front of the camera — no typing needed.

    • ☝️ Point move the cursor and push particles
    • 🤏 Pinch grab and turn, or click buttons
    • ✊ Fist pull the particles in
    • ✋ Open hand after a fist: explode!
    • ✌️ Peace change colours / style
    • ↕️ Closer / farther move your hand to zoom
    • 👋 Swipe fast left / right: change scene
    • ☝️ Point at a tab hold it there for 1 second to open it

    The camera stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
