    Particle 3D

    Interactive particle worlds you control with your hand.
    Hold your hand up in front of the camera — no typing needed.

    • ☝️ Point move the cursor & push particles
    • 🤏 Pinch grab & rotate, or click buttons
    • ✊ Fist pull particles in
    • ✋ Open palm let them go / explode
    • 👋 Swipe left / right to change scene

    The camera stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
